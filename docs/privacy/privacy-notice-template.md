# Privacy notice template

POPIA section 18 requires the institution to tell people what personal information it
collects and why. Adapt this template, have it approved by the institution's information
officer, and publish it where users sign in (for example linked from the institution's
intranet or the sign-in email). Replace everything in `[brackets]`. Check it against the
[data inventory](data-inventory.md) after each OCMP upgrade.

!!! note "Not legal advice"
    This is a starting point, not legal advice. The institution is responsible for its
    notice.

---

## Privacy notice: [System name, e.g. "Curriculum Mapping (OCMP)"]

**Responsible party:** [Institution name], [physical address].
**Information officer:** [name or title], [email address], [telephone].
**Last updated:** [date].

### What this system is

[System name] holds the [institution]'s curricula: programmes, modules, sessions, learning
objectives and how they map to competency frameworks. Staff use it to plan and review
curricula; [students use it to look up what is taught where].

### What personal information we collect

- Your name and work email address.
- If you sign in with a password: a one-way protected form of your password (we cannot
  read it).
- If you sign in with your [institution] account: an identifier from [identity provider,
  e.g. Microsoft Entra ID] that links you to your account.
- The roles you have been given and for which programmes or modules.
- A record of the changes you make to curriculum, framework and mapping data, and when.
- Technical information: when you last signed in, and your device's IP address and browser
  type in server logs.

We do not collect student marks, registration, health or other student records in this
system.

### Where it comes from

From [the system administrators / your institution account / you].

### Why we use it

- To let you sign in and to give you the access your role needs.
- To show who made or reviewed curriculum changes, for quality assurance and
  accreditation (for example [CHE / HPCSA]) reviews.
- To keep the system secure, including limiting repeated sign-in attempts.
- To send you emails about your account, such as password reset links.

We process this information because it is necessary for [your employment / your studies /
the institution's legitimate interest in managing and quality-assuring its curricula]
[information officer to confirm the lawful basis under POPIA section 11].

Providing your information is [required to use the system].

### Who receives it

- Other users of the system see your name next to the work you have done.
- [Name of hosting operator, if not the institution] hosts the system on our behalf under
  a written agreement.
- Our email service [provider] sends account emails.
- [If AI features are used:] curriculum text is sent to [AI provider, country] to suggest
  mappings. Your personal information is never sent to it.

Your information [is / is not] transferred outside South Africa [details and safeguards].

### How long we keep it

- Your account: while you need access, and [period] after you leave.
- Change history (audit log): [period, matching `AUDIT_LOG_RETENTION_DAYS`].
- Server logs: [period].
- Backups: [period].

When your account is removed, your name is removed from the change history; the
curriculum records themselves are kept.

### How we protect it

The system runs on [institution]-controlled servers, uses encrypted connections,
stores passwords only in protected form, limits access by role, and records
administrators' views of personal information.

### Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or
delete it, and object to its processing, by contacting [information officer contact].
If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Information Regulator
of South Africa ([check the Regulator's website for current contact details]).
