# Guide: lecturer or facilitator

You teach in a module or a session. You can see the parts of the curriculum you work on and edit the learning
objectives of your own sessions. You cannot change the structure, publish, or map.

## What you can do

1. **Find your sessions.** Open **Curriculum**. A role at a module shows that module with its sessions; a role at
   one session shows just that session under its module's name. A note says you see only the parts you work on.
2. **Write or change your session objectives.** Select your session; use **Add**, **Edit** or **Delete** next to
   its objectives. You can link each objective to the module outcomes (and programme outcomes) it supports, and
   set its domain (knowledge, skills, attitudes) and taxonomy level. Details: [Who can edit
   objectives](../admin/users-and-roles.md#who-can-edit-objectives).
3. **Use the wording check.** As you type, the checker flags vague verbs ("understand", "know"), several verbs in
   one objective, a missing domain or upward link, and very long objectives, and suggests a level from the verb.
   It is advice: it never stops you saving. See [Objective quality
   checker](../admin/users-and-roles.md#objective-quality-checker).
4. **Read the published curriculum** in **Explorer** and **Search**, as students do.

## Good to know

* If you reword an objective that is already approved, it goes back to *ready for review* so the module
  coordinator can approve it again. Approving needs a coordinator or above.
* Published and archived versions are read-only. Changes go into the draft for the next year.
* The AI help (rewrite, suggest a level) and study-guide reading need the *AI run* permission, which lecturers do
  not hold. Ask your module coordinator.
