# Guide: external reviewer

You are an outside examiner or accreditor with **time-limited, read-only** access to selected programmes. Your
access has an end date.

## What you can do

1. **Read the published curriculum** in **Explorer** and **Search**: the structure, sessions, objectives and
   assessments of a published year.
2. **Read the reports** of your programme (coverage, gaps, traceability and others), with each report's method
   and date printed on it. Exports are not available to external reviewers.

## What you cannot do

* Change anything.
* See drafts, other programmes, or people's accounts.
* Download the full data exports.

Evidence packs (a bundle for an accreditation visit) are planned for a later release; your role already carries
the permission for them.

If your access has ended or something expected is missing, ask the person who invited you. More detail: [Users
and roles](../admin/users-and-roles.md#built-in-roles).
