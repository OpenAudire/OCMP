# Guide: mapper and reviewer

You connect learning objectives to the competency frameworks and check the connections others (or the AI)
propose. A mapper role is held at a programme or a module.

## What you can do

1. **Make links by hand.** In **Mapping**, choose an objective on the left and a framework item on the right,
   then **Link**. Give each link a **coverage** from 0 to 5 and a **depth** (below, matches, above, not
   applicable); the **?** button explains the scale. Edit or remove your links. Only draft versions can be mapped.
   See [The mapping workspace](../admin/mapping.md).
2. **Ask the AI for suggestions** for a programme or a module when AI is switched on. They arrive as
   *suggested* links; none is accepted by itself.
3. **Work through the Review queue.** For each waiting link, read the objective, the framework item, the reason and
   the evidence quote, then **accept** (A), **reject** (R), **edit** the coverage or depth (E), **skip** (S) or
   **send to second review** (2). Every action is also a button. The number beside *Review queue* in the menu is
   how many you can act on. See [The review queue](../admin/review-queue.md).
4. **Accept confident suggestions in bulk.** *Accept all N and above* handles first-review suggestions at or above
   a confidence floor your institution sets, up to a cap. Bulk-accepted links stay marked as such in reports.
5. **Second review** (four-eyes mode): a different person confirms or disagrees; disagreements are flagged for
   discussion.
6. **Re-check.** A link whose objective's wording changed is flagged; press **Still fits** or fix it.

## Good to know

* You cannot give the second review of a link you reviewed first or made yourself.
* Single-key shortcuts work only while focus is in the queue and can be switched off.
