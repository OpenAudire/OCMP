# Guide: programme director

You own a programme: its outcomes, its structure year by year, and publishing. The role is held **at the
programme**.

## What you can do

1. **Build the structure.** In **Curriculum**: phases, modules and sessions (add, edit, drag or use **Move up /
   Move down**, copy), and the programme outcomes. See [Who can change the curriculum
   structure](../admin/users-and-roles.md#who-can-change-the-curriculum-structure).
2. **Copy a year forward.** From the version menu, make next year's draft from this year's version (keeping
   mappings and their review state, optionally the role assignments).
3. **Compare versions** to see what changed between two years.
4. **Check credits and notional hours** per module, per year and against the NQF level
   ([Assessments and credits](../admin/assessments-and-credits.md)).
5. **Publish** a draft once it has at least one module; **return a published version to draft** with a written
   reason when it must be corrected; **archive** an old year ([Publishing and copying a year
   forward](../admin/users-and-roles.md#publishing-and-copying-a-year-forward)).
6. **Oversee the mapping.** Use **Mapping**, the **Review queue** and, for the committee, the **Dashboard**
   ([Committee dashboard](../admin/committee-dashboard.md)).
7. **Read the reports** and export them to Excel or PDF: coverage, gaps, redundancy, traceability, teaching
   formats, role balance, assessment methods ([Coverage and gap reports](../admin/coverage-reports.md),
   [Redundancy and traceability](../admin/curriculum-reports.md), [Teaching reports](../admin/teaching-reports.md)).
8. **Import and export** the curriculum with Excel ([Importing the curriculum](../admin/importing-curriculum.md),
   [Exporting a version](../admin/exporting.md)).
9. **Give roles** inside your programme to people who hold at least those permissions.

## Good to know

* Every report states its method and its date. Read [Reading the reports with
  care](../admin/coverage-reports.md#reading-the-reports-with-care) before quoting a figure.
