# Guide: module coordinator

You look after one or more modules: their sessions, outcomes, assessments, tags, and the mapping of their
objectives. Your role is held **at the module**, so you see and change that module only.

## What you can do

1. **Shape the module.** In **Curriculum**, add, edit, move and copy the module's sessions, and set the module's
   own details (credits, level, hours). Structure above the module (phases, adding modules) is the programme
   director's. See [Who can change the curriculum
   structure](../admin/users-and-roles.md#who-can-change-the-curriculum-structure).
2. **Write the module outcomes** and approve objectives. You can set an objective to *approved* or *retired*;
   lecturers can only draft and send for review.
3. **Add assessments** and link each question or part to the objectives it assesses. Weights are advice, not
   forced to add up. See [Assessments and credits](../admin/assessments-and-credits.md).
4. **Check credits and hours** for the module against its credits (advice only).
5. **Tag** the module and its sessions with the institution's tag lists, with a preview before the tag is passed
   to the sessions below ([Tag lists and tagging](../admin/tag-lists.md)).
6. **Map your objectives** to framework items in **Mapping**, or ask the AI for suggestions for your module when
   AI is switched on ([The mapping workspace](../admin/mapping.md)).
7. **Review.** In the **Review queue** you review suggestions inside your module, and give second reviews there
   (never of a link you reviewed first) ([The review queue](../admin/review-queue.md)).
8. **Read your module's reports** where you have report access.
9. **Read a study guide into proposed objectives** (AI on): you confirm it holds no patient information, then
   accept or reject each proposal ([Study guides](../admin/users-and-roles.md#study-guides)).

## Good to know

* You edit **draft** versions only. A published year is changed by the programme director returning it to draft,
  with a written reason.
* A link whose objective wording changed is flagged *needs a re-check*; press **Still fits** or change it.
