# Guide: student

You see the **published** curriculum only: what is taught, when, and what each assessment covers. You cannot
change anything, and nothing here is about you personally (no marks, no timetable).

## What you can do

1. **See what is taught this week.** Open **Explorer**. *By week* lists the sessions of one teaching week across
   your modules, with the format, the time with the teacher and in self-study, and what you should be able to do
   by the end of each session. **Earlier** and **Later** move between weeks. Weeks are week numbers, not dates.
2. **Read a module.** In *Modules*, open a module to see what it covers, its sessions week by week, and its
   **assessments**: the method, the weight, the timing, and what each one covers. Open *questions or parts* to
   see what each question assesses.
3. **Search.** **Search** finds objectives, sessions and modules by words and, when your institution has the
   meaning-based search on, by meaning. "Where do we teach this?" lists the sessions that teach a topic in order.
4. **See where the year builds a competency (only if your institution turned it on).** A *Competencies* view lists
   each competency group and the modules and sessions of this year that help you build it. It shows where it is
   taught, not how well, and has no scores.

## If something is missing

* *No published curriculum yet*: the curriculum office has not published this year. Ask them when it will be.
* A module or session you expect is not listed: you may not have the *Student* role for your programme.
