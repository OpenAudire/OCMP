# Guide: curriculum office

You look after what is shared: frameworks, tag lists, versions and reports, across the institution or one
faculty. You also hold everything a programme director holds, for the programmes in your scope.

## What you can do

1. **Load and browse frameworks.** In **Frameworks**, import a framework pack (JSON) or an Excel workbook, check
   the preview, then confirm. Browse a framework as a tree and see where each item is mapped. Blank templates
   (HPCSA, NQF, graduate attributes, MeSH) hold structure only, never official text; fill them from the
   authoritative source. See [The framework library](../admin/frameworks.md) and [Importing
   frameworks](../admin/framework-packs.md).
2. **Keep tag lists.** Create institution-wide lists (or a faculty's own) such as teaching methods and assessment
   methods; a list or tag in use is made inactive, not deleted ([Tag lists](../admin/tag-lists.md)).
3. **Run the reports** for any programme in scope and export them ([Coverage and gap
   reports](../admin/coverage-reports.md), [Teaching reports](../admin/teaching-reports.md),
   [Redundancy and traceability](../admin/curriculum-reports.md)).
4. **Prepare the committee** with the [dashboard](../admin/committee-dashboard.md): indicators with trends,
   traceability, credits.
5. **Import and export in bulk.** Excel for structure and objectives, tags, mappings and frameworks, with a
   preview and dry run before anything is written; the full round-trip export, the objectives-by-framework
   matrix and a JSON of a version ([Importing the curriculum](../admin/importing-curriculum.md), [Tags, mappings and
   frameworks](../admin/importing-tags-mappings-frameworks.md), [Exporting](../admin/exporting.md)).
6. **Publish and archive** versions with the programme directors.
7. **Switch on the student competency view** only if your administrator has agreed that the year's mapping is
   reviewed enough to show students ([The curriculum explorer](../admin/explorer.md#competencies-optional)).

## Good to know

* Imports never delete. A mapping import only suggests links unless you tick *decided* and may review; those
  links are accepted with no reviewer recorded and are flagged in reports.
